# Privacy Policy for Cozy Pets 🐾

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026  

Cozy Pets ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and data protection.

---

## 🔒 1. Zero Data Collection

Cozy Pets operates **100% locally** within your browser. 

- We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).
- We **do not** track your browsing history, visited websites, clicks, or keystrokes.
- We **do not** send any data to external servers, analytics services, or third parties.

---

## 💾 2. Local Storage Usage

The extension uses the browser's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) strictly to save your local extension preferences on your device:

- Pet nickname preference
- Sound mute/unmute state and volume settings
- Selected pet coat skin
- Gamified bond/likeness points

This data never leaves your personal device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing browser extension data.

---

## 🛡️ 3. Third-Party Access

Since no user data is collected or processed, zero user data is ever sold, rented, or transferred to third parties for advertising or any other purposes.

---

## 🌐 4. Permissions Disclosure

- **`storage`**: Used exclusively for saving user customization preferences on the user's local machine.
